# Mechanic changes

For any mechanic that's not described in here, assume Generation 9 mechanics.

### General

Battle style is forced to set. 
Bag is inaccessible during trainer battles. 
Gym Trainers and Gym Leaders will heal your party to full before battle.
EVs are removed from the game.
TMs are consumed on use.
HMs do not need to be taught, obtaining them and having the required badge for use is enough.
Moves that are 1 PP for Player are 8 PP for AI (except Revival Blessing).

Critical hit chance for AI: 1/8 on Stage 0, 1/4 on Stage 1. 
Critical hit chance for Player: 1/16 on Stage 0, 1/8 on Stage 1. 
All remaining Stages are unchanged. These changes are not applied in Casual Mode.

Paralysis: Speed decreases by 75%.
Sleep: If a Pokémon enters a battle asleep it's sleeping turns count is reset.
Confusion: If inflicted by Berserk Gene, Self-Hit Power of 60 instead of 40.

Illuminate: Intimidate for Special Attack.
Leaf Guard, Magma Armor: Prevents critical hits on top of it's existing effects.
Battle Bond: Transforms Greninja into Ash-Greninja upon fainting a Pokémon.
Libero: Changes type every turn.
Tera Shift: Transforms into Stellar form if level 80 or above (AI only).

Terrain damage boost: Electric/Grass/Psychic Terrains will boost damage of moves of the matching type by 50%.
Terrain removal: Not removed by Defog or Ice Spinner.

Held items: Items that get consumed or removed from your Pokémon in any way during battle will not be restored.
Confuse inducing berries: Restore half HP, triggering at 1/4 HP.
Micle Berry: 1.5 Accuracy Boost instead of 1.2.

Safari Balls: 100% catch rate.

Berries cannot be planted.
No Pokémon take overworld damage from being poisoned.
Fishing always guaranteed to reel without precise timing.

Rotom Form Changing:
Birch's Lab now has appliances for Rotom.
Rotom-Wash will receive Surf over Hydro Pump.
Rotom-Frost will receive Ice Beam over Blizzard.
Rotom-Fan will receive Aeroblast over Air Slash.
When Rotom recalls into its base form, it's special Move will always be replaced with Shadow Ball.

### IVs

-Baby and Legendary Pokémon no longer have 3 IVs set to 31
-The Starter Pokémon and Type: Null have 3 IVs set to 31
-Traded Pokémon have 2 IVs set to 31

### Comeback mechanic

The comeback mechanic is designed to help Nuzlocke runs recover from heavy losses. Throughout the journey, players who have suffered enough deaths can receive assistance from Anabel and Hunter in Route 124.

Activation Requirements and Rewards:
-After obtaining the Stone Badge, players with at least 3 deaths can speak to Anabel in Root Academy to receive one random Level 20 Pokémon from the following pool: Arbok, Blitzle, Boltund, Dubwool, Espurr, Lilligant, Mudsdale, Ninetales-Alola, Swalot, Tyrogue, or Vespiquen.
-After obtaining the Balance Badge, players with at least 12 deaths can speak to Anabel in Root Academy to receive one random Level 20 Pokémon from the following pool: Accelgor, Braviary, Breloom, Carracosta, Cofagrigus, Crabominable, Falinks, Galvantula, Sigilyph, Tauros-Paldea-Combat, or Trevenant.
-All Pokémon gifted by Anabel have Root Academy as their met location. Some may come with their Scale moves.
-Later in the game, players who have accumulated at least 30 deaths can speak to Hunter on Route 124 before defeating Leaf in Mossdeep to receive additional rewards. However, at least one of Anabel's two Comeback flags must have been activated for this reward to be available.

Hunter's Rewards:
-1 Master Ball
-1 randomly chosen Mint: Hasty or Naive
-1 random gift Pokémon holding an Eject Button: Sceptile, Weavile, Sneasler, Ursaluna, Tapu Koko, Volcarona, Aegislash, Aerodactyl, or Starmie.

#### Death counter
The death counter tracks Pokémon that faint during Trainer Battles only. Faints during wild encounters do not count.
